# Northeast New Orleans Elevation & Demographic Study

## Project Overview
This project presents a geospatial analysis of Northeast New Orleans using elevation and demographic mapping techniques. The analysis combines terrain elevation, flood risk indicators, transportation infrastructure, and population characteristics to better understand environmental vulnerability and community distribution across the region.

The project includes two primary GIS-derived visualizations:

1. **Elevation & Flood Risk Map**
2. **Population & Demographic Distribution Maps**

These visualizations support urban planning, disaster preparedness, infrastructure resilience, and demographic analysis.

---

# Elevation & Flood Risk Analysis

## Elevation Map
The elevation map visualizes terrain height relative to water level and levee systems throughout Northeast New Orleans.

### Key Features
- Elevation ranges represented with graduated color symbology
- Mississippi River and nearby waterways
- Highway transportation routes
- Hospital locations
- Levee references and flood-prone zones

<img width="1000" alt="image" src="https://github.com/JamesA-usa/New-Orleans-Study/blob/main/Elevation_Map.png">




### Elevation Categories

| Elevation Range | Description |
|---|---|
| -3.5 to 0 feet | Below Mississippi River level |
| 0 to 3 feet | Below Lake Pontchartrain levels |
| 3 to 5 feet | Below New Orleans river levees level |
| 5 to 7 feet | Near levee elevations |
| 7+ feet | Higher elevation terrain |

## Analytical Insights
- Large portions of Northeast New Orleans remain at or below surrounding water levels.
- Critical infrastructure, including hospitals and transportation corridors, intersect with low-lying terrain.
- The meandering river system and levee proximity significantly influence regional flood vulnerability.
- Elevation disparities highlight areas requiring enhanced flood mitigation and emergency planning.

## Potential Applications
- Flood risk assessment
- Emergency management planning
- Urban resilience studies
- Infrastructure protection analysis
- Environmental justice research

# Population & Demographic Analysis

## Population Distribution Map
The population visualization explores demographic and spatial population characteristics across Northeast New Orleans census blocks.

### Included Demographic Layers
- Total population by census block
- Male population percentage
- Population density per square kilometer


<img width="1000" alt="image" src="https://github.com/JamesA-usa/New-Orleans-Study/blob/main/Population_Map.jpeg">

## Population Insights

### Total Population
- Higher concentrations of residents are clustered along major transportation and developed corridors.
- Peripheral and environmentally constrained areas show lower residential density.

### Male Population Percentage
- Demographic variation exists across census blocks.
- Some regions exhibit significantly higher male population ratios, potentially reflecting housing patterns or workforce distributions.

### Population Density
- Dense urbanized areas are concentrated near infrastructure networks and developed neighborhoods.
- Lower-density zones correspond with wetlands, industrial land, or flood-prone terrain.
